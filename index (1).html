About

I started in software — writing compilers, building web applications, working through the usual Computer Science curriculum. That background is still the foundation of how I think: in systems, data flow, and edge cases.

Somewhere along the way, the more interesting question stopped being how you build something and became how you watch it — what it looks like when something goes wrong. That's what pulled me toward Security Operations: logs, traffic, network behavior, and the discipline of noticing what doesn't belong.

I'm not there through job experience yet — I'm there through structured training (DEPI, Cisco Networking Academy) and consistent hands-on lab practice with tools like Wireshark, Packet Tracer, and GNS3. I'd rather be upfront about that than dress it up.

Where I'm headed

I help security teams get an analyst who already reads networks and systems fluently — built through Computer Science fundamentals, networking training, and hands-on lab practice with real monitoring and analysis tools.

Shorter version: a CS student who understands how systems are built, now training to understand how they're monitored and defended.
